STRUCTURED CARE METHODOLOGY

What actually happens in therapy?

No ambiguous mystical jargon. We break personal change into clear, structured milestones.

MILESTONE 01 STAGE 1

We talk.

INITIAL HANDSHAKE

Not “tell me about your childhood.” We start with what is happening in your life right now. We trace origin dependencies later once trust is established.

MILESTONE 02 STAGE 2

We find patterns.

DEPENDENCY TRACING

What triggers the reaction? What keeps reinforcing the response? We map: input → reaction → consequence → repeat to pinpoint the loop.

MILESTONE 03 STAGE 3

We test changes.

BEHAVIORAL DIFFS

Small, controlled adjustments to communication, boundary setting, and self-interpretation. No complete personality rewrite required.

MILESTONE 04 STAGE 4

You keep what works.

STABLE EMOTIONAL RELEASE

Integrate sustainable emotional defaults into everyday life. Breaking changes are strictly optional; durable stability is the true target.
