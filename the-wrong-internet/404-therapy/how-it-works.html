THE PROTOCOL

Therapy without the weird mystery.

Demystifying the clinical process. Here is what actually happens across each stage of your care.

STAGE 01 ● INITIAL HANDSHAKE

15-Minute Zero-Commitment Discovery Call

A low-friction connection to ensure mutual fit. You outline what’s happening, ask about the therapist’s background, and confirm whether you feel understood. We verify clinical suitability before any fee is billed.

Payload: 15 min · Free of charge · Secure Video Link
STAGE 02 ● DEPENDENCY TRACE

Mapping the Loops & Root Triggers (Sessions 1–4)

We don’t spend four weeks reciting your life history like a monologue. We analyze the recurring patterns happening this week, examine the emotional inputs, and trace where the automated coping scripts were first installed.

Payload: Weekly 50-min sessions · Intake Diagnostic Assessment
STAGE 03 ● BEHAVIORAL DIFFS

Testing Small Behavioral Patches (Sessions 5–16)

This is the core iterative work. We introduce small, safe behavioral diffs: setting a boundary without an apology, tolerating uncertainty for 20 minutes without Googling, or having a direct conflict conversation without retreating into defensiveness.

Payload: Micro-experiments between sessions · Continuous feedback
STAGE 04 ● STABLE RELEASE

Sustainable Emotional Defaults & Maintenance

The goal of good therapy is to make therapy unnecessary. Once healthy emotional regulation and communication loops are self-sustaining, we transition to bi-weekly check-ins or conclude care with an open-door policy for future maintenance.

Payload: Gradual step-down · Long-term resilience
