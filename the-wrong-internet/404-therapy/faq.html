If a recurring emotional reaction, relationship conflict, or work depletion pattern keeps occurring despite your best conscious efforts to fix it with logic, therapy provides a dedicated external sandbox to trace what is sustaining the loop.
No. You do not need a DSM diagnosis to benefit from therapy. Many of our clients are high-functioning professionals dealing with perfectionism, boundary exhaustion, life transitions, or relationship communication breakdowns.
We begin with what is happening in your life today. Your therapist will ask targeted questions to understand current stressors, history of previous coping mechanisms, and what a successful outcome looks like to you.
Individual 50-minute clinical sessions are $225–$275 depending on clinician level of licensure. Couples 75-minute sessions are $325. Initial 15-minute handshake discovery calls are always free.
We operate as an out-of-network practice to maintain complete clinical autonomy and uncompromising privacy. We provide itemized monthly superbills with all necessary diagnostic and procedure codes, which clients submit to their PPO insurance for typically 60–80% out-of-network reimbursement.
Relational chemistry is the single highest predictor of clinical efficacy. If you don't feel the right resonance, we actively support seamless re-routing to another clinician on our team without awkwardness or guilt.
