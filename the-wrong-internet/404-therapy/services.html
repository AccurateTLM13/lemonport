SPECIALTY TRACKS

Targeted clinical pathways.

We don’t treat human beings like generic monoliths. We isolate the specific subsystem loops causing emotional throttling.

TRACK 01 // ANXIETY_LOOP ● HIGH CONCURRENCY

High-Functioning Anxiety & Preemptive Error Handling

Your brain opened 37 tabs again. You prepare for every disastrous edge case before lunchtime, exhaust yourself before noon, and call it “staying organized.”

OBSERVED PATTERNS

  • • Perpetual worst-case scenario forecasting
  • • Difficulty relaxing without feeling guilty
  • • Somatic tension (chest tightness, shallow breathing)
  • • Perfectionism as a protective armor against criticism

CLINICAL INTERVENTION

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT) to demystify catastrophic thoughts, reduce background task overload, and calibrate healthy uncertainty tolerance.

Recommended Lead: Dr. Maya Chen Schedule for Anxiety [↵]
TRACK 02 // BURNOUT_WARNING ● THERMAL THROTTLING

Chronic Depletion & Executive Exhaustion

You are technically still running. Deliverables get completed, but creative curiosity is gone and you operate on battery backup power.

OBSERVED PATTERNS

  • • Cynicism toward things you once found meaningful
  • • Emotional detachment and cognitive brain fog
  • • Revenge bedtime procrastination
  • • Feeling completely spent yet unable to truly recharge

CLINICAL INTERVENTION

Occupational psychology and nervous system regulation. We establish rigid firewall boundaries around non-working hours and deconstruct toxic over-responsibility defaults.

Recommended Lead: Dr. Elena Rostova Schedule for Burnout [↵]
TRACK 03 // RELATIONSHIP_CONFLICT ● PACKET LOSS

Systemic Relationship Loops & Attachment Friction

Same argument. New timestamp. You debate surface logistics (chores, schedules, tone) while competing attachment wounds fuel the underlying conflict.

OBSERVED PATTERNS

  • • Stonewalling or aggressive withdrawal during disputes
  • • Unspoken expectations leading to resentment
  • • Anxious pursuit vs. avoidant shutdown dynamics
  • • Fear that asserting personal needs will cause abandonment

CLINICAL INTERVENTION

Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) and Family Systems Theory. We map the relational feedback loop to teach real-time de-escalation and direct, non-defensive vulnerability.

Recommended Lead: Marcus Vance, LMFT Schedule for Relationships [↵]
