Licenses

Clear rights, or it does not enter the booth

Every track on Lemonteed FM needs verified usage rights before it gets listed. A track being free to hear does not automatically mean it is free to reuse.

Attribution, license links, and source terms may matter. Open the source page before using a track in your own project.

Free-use does not mean free-for-all.

Lemonteed FM shows license info where available, but users should verify the original source before using a track in their own project.

About

A tiny player, not a music empire

Lemonteed FM is a static discovery page for tracks with visible license information. It is built for quick listening, copying attribution, and remembering that "free" is not a legal strategy.
