About

A Tiny Player, Not a Music Empire

LemonteedFM is a tiny discovery player for unusual tracks, clear usage information, and music that fell through the cracks. It is not a streaming empire. It is one shiny lemon with access to a transmitter.

Broadcasting from the internet's least funded booth. Songs the algorithm forgot. Verified license. Unverified vibes. The catalog is small by design, updated slowly by someone with specific opinions and no professional authority whatsoever.
