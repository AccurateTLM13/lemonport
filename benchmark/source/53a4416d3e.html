D-01 / Cinema Story

Some films deserve a room, a print, and a specific hour.

Analog Cinema Club screens exclusively from physical media. 35mm prints, restored DCPs, and rare 4K masters are the only formats we project. We believe the conditions of viewing are part of the work itself.

Our programming is built around director retrospectives, thematic series, and midnight screenings of films that benefit from a dark room and an attentive audience. We do not stream. We do not release windows.

The theater operates three screens in a converted warehouse near the river. Memberships fund our print preservation work and grant early access to limited-run screenings.
