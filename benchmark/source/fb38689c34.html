D-01 / LEAGUE STORY

After work, the lights come on.

Since 1938, local businesses have put their names on jerseys and their neighbors on the field. The league grew around four municipal diamonds, volunteer grounds crews, and Friday evenings that belong to the whole town.

Grandparents keep score beside children chasing foul balls. The annual All-Star break closes Main Street. Competition matters here because community does.
