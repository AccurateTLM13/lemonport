ENGINEERING STATEMENT

Relay is built on the conviction that autonomous AI agent systems should not be deployed through opaque, glossy dashboards with vague status indicators. As AI agents take on critical operational responsibilities, managing them requires the uncompromising rigor and verification discipline of industrial instrumentation.

Every agent configuration, tool authorization boundary, and runtime state in Relay is treated as a verifiable operational document. Changes are recorded in immutable revision ledgers, execution stages must be explicitly signed off, and system telemetry resolves through deterministic mechanical states.

By uniting the tactile precision of the Solari split-flap mechanism with modern AI runtime orchestration, Relay provides teams with total visibility, auditability, and command over their autonomous software fleets.
