07 / PUBLIC REFERENCE

Public reference

The field system is operational by design. This section translates the same work into the questions visitors most often ask.

07.1 / Closures

Why are trails temporarily closed?

A temporary closure is used when a trail segment cannot be kept reliably passable while work is underway or while a condition is being evaluated. Common reasons include storm damage, bridge or drainage repair, unstable tread, fallen trees, or work that requires equipment to occupy the full trail width.

07.2 / Inspection

What do trail crews inspect?

Crews check access points, the travel surface, drainage, signs and markers, vegetation clearance, structures, and any condition that could interrupt normal use. They also record changes so the next inspection can be compared with the previous one.

07.3 / Priority

How are maintenance priorities determined?

Conditions that block access or create an immediate route problem are addressed first. After that, supervisors consider severity, how quickly a condition may worsen, trail use, crew availability, weather, and whether several repairs can be completed efficiently in the same area.

07.4 / Reporting

How can visitors report an issue?

Include the trail name or number, the nearest marker or landmark, what you observed, and when you saw it. A photo can help when it is safe to take one. Reports are reviewed by trail services and matched to an existing inspection or a new field order.
