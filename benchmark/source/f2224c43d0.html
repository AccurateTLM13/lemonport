PUBLIC REF

Public Operational Guide

CIVIC POLICY REFERENCE

07.1 / Why Trails Are Temporarily Closed

Trail closures are enacted exclusively when physical conditions compromise visitor safety or when continued use would cause irreversible ecological and structural damage. During severe precipitation or thaw events, saturated soil tread loses structural bearing capacity. Heavy foot or bike traffic under these conditions cuts deep ruts through the protective top layer, turning natural drainage pathways into erosive gullies. Temporary closures allow the subgrade to consolidate, protecting taxpayer investment in trail infrastructure.

07.2 / What Trail Crews Inspect

District trail rangers conduct systematic physical audits across all 142 miles of network. Inspections evaluate five structural domains: Tread Integrity (monitoring compaction and roots), Drainage Structures (testing culverts and waterbars for hydraulic flow), Corridor Clearance (maintaining an 8-foot vertical brush envelope), Structural Anchors (inspecting footbridges, boardwalks, and timber cribbing), and Safety Signage (verifying legibility of boundary and warning placards).

07.3 / How Maintenance Priorities Are Determined

Dispatches are categorized into three operational priority tiers. Priority 1 (Immediate Hazard) includes active structural failures, downed trees obstructing primary thoroughfares, or severe washouts threatening public safety. Priority 2 (Scheduled Corrective Maintenance) addresses functional degradation such as clogged culverts or surface rutting before severe damage occurs. Priority 3 (Routine Maintenance) covers scheduled corridor clearing, sign updates, and minor tread re-grading.

07.4 / How Visitors Can Report an Issue

Public observations provide vital ground data for dispatchers. When reporting a field condition, note the exact trail ID and nearest milepost marker (found on all brown post signages at 0.2-mile intervals). Describe the obstruction clearly (e.g., "Downed birch branch 3 inches diameter" or "Silted culvert intake"). Reports can be submitted via the public reporting terminal below or by calling District Central Dispatch at 555-0194 (Ext. 3).
