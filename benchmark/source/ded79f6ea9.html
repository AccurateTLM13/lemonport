D-01 / ENGINEERING STORY

Distance changes the brief.

The R7 exists for riders who stay out long enough to notice every compromise. Its geometry distributes load between hands and saddle while keeping the front wheel precise on technical descents.

Wide-tire clearance, replaceable cockpit parts, and standard bearing interfaces are not concessions. They are the structure of a faster bicycle that remains useful after the launch season is over.
