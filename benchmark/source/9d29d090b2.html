D-01 / LEAGUE STORY

After work, the lights come on.

The Municipal League has been part of this town for three generations. Local businesses sponsor teams. Families bring folding chairs to neighborhood fields. Volunteer grounds crews maintain the diamonds before Friday night games.

This is not professional baseball. It is something more honest — a civic institution built on community, competition, and the simple pleasure of watching the game under lights after a week of work.
