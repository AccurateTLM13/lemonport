Independent radio for the hours nobody owns.

Night Shift broadcasts from midnight until sunrise, filling the frequencies that commercial stations leave dark. We play music without format constraints, host conversations that would not survive daylight, and record live sessions in a studio barely larger than the booth itself.

The station was built on the belief that the overnight hours deserve their own culture, not the reruns of morning programming, but a distinct voice for the people awake by choice or by necessity. Truck drivers, night nurses, students, insomniacs, and intentional wanderers all find something here that does not exist at noon.

We fund ourselves through listener support and the occasional grant from people who remember what independent media used to mean. No advertisements after midnight. No algorithms. Just a board operator, a host, and whoever is listening.
