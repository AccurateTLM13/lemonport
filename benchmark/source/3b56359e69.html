ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE

The Halloway HX-4 Environmental Field Monitor is a self-contained precision instrument for remote hydrological, atmospheric, and pedological telemetry. Built within a CNC-machined anodized aluminum monocoque, the unit operates in extreme ambient conditions without requiring external environmental shelters.

Equipped with a dual-redundant sensor bus, active thermistor compensation, and direct satellite/cellular sub-GHz packet broadcast, the HX-4 provides continuous field verification for surveyor teams and environmental research units.

SECTION 06 // MANDATORY ADVISORIES

SERVICE NOTICES & SAFETY PROCEDURES

[ CAUTION // REF-CAUT-HX4-082 ] SEALING INTEGRITY CRITICAL

Ensure M12 soil interface connector threads are completely free of particulate matter before coupling. Sand or fine silica grit on the nitrile O-ring seal will compromise IP68 water resistance during deep soil submergence (>0.5m hydrostatic depth).

MANDATORY REMEDY: Inspect O-ring with 10x optical loupe prior to mating. Apply Fluorosilicone grease (Part #HW-LUB-02) every 50 mating cycles.

[ NOTE // REF-NOTE-HX4-019 ] COLD WEATHER OPERATION

When deploying in ambient temperatures below -20°C (-4°F), allow the GNSS module 90 seconds to execute cold-start ephemeris acquisition before logging coordinate fixes. Lithium Iron Phosphate battery capacity diminishes by approximately 12% at -30°C.

RECOMMENDED ACTION: Enable internal thermostatic pulse heater mode in sub-zero deployments to maintain peak discharge voltage.
