05 / Field note 18

Skillet Corn with Cultured Butter & Charred Pepper.

Ingredients / four

  • 6 ears sweet corn
  • 3 tbsp cultured butter
  • 2 charred sweet peppers
  • 1 small shallot
  • Cider vinegar + salt
  • Basil to finish

Preparation

Cut the kernels from the cob. Brown the butter in a wide skillet, add shallot and corn, then leave undisturbed long enough to color. Fold through torn pepper, vinegar and salt. Finish with basil while warm.
