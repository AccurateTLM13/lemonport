Work cell

A cell that can be moved when the line moves.

CELL 04 is a six-axis modular work cell for small and mid-sized manufacturers. Payload, reach, and repeatability are fixed. The cell’s place in the factory is not.

The same module can be specified, placed, and later reconfigured as production demands evolve.

Three capabilities
01

Modular cells

Work cells are built as modules, not as fixtures welded to a single layout. CELL 04 deploys as a unit that can be relocated.

02

Reconfiguration

As production demands evolve, the cell can be reconfigured rather than replaced.

03

Mid-scale manufacture

Specified for small and mid-sized manufacturers: 35 kg, 1,850 mm, six-axis, ±0.05 mm.

Deployment

Specify · Place · Run · Adapt

  1. 01

    Specify

    Match CELL 04 to the work: 35 kg payload, 1,850 mm reach, six-axis motion, modular deployment.

  2. 02

    Place

    Install the cell as a module. It occupies a position; it is not assumed to occupy that position forever.

  3. 03

    Run

    Operate within the published envelope: six axes, 35 kg, 1,850 mm, ±0.05 mm repeatability.

  4. 04

    Adapt

    When the factory changes, reconfigure the cell. The product is built for lines that do not stay still.
