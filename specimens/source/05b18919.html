ORIGIN

Huila, Colombia

High-altitude farms in the Colombian Andes produce some of the most complex coffees in the world. Huila's volcanic soil and microclimates create conditions for extraordinary sweetness and clarity.

2°32′N / 75°32′W

Las Flores

A family-owned farm managed by the Velasco family. Production is small-lot, hand-picked, and processed at origin. Each harvest is cupped and scored before selection.

ALTITUDE / 1,850 MASL

Washed Process

Cherries are depulped, fermented for 18–24 hours, then washed and dried on raised beds. The washed process produces a clean, transparent cup with pronounced acidity.

FERMENT / 18–24 HRS
ROASTING PHILOSOPHY
