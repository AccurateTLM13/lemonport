PRESERVATION MISSION

The Great Lakes built America’s industrial backbone. These records are how we remember the hands that built it.

The Great Lakes Industrial Archive was founded in 1987 to preserve the material record of manufacturing in the Great Lakes region. Our collection spans more than a century of industrial activity, from early foundries to modern automated production.
