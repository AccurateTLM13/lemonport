06 / field note

Skillet Corn
with Cultured Butter
& Charred Pepper

  • 6 ears sweet corn
  • 2 tbsp cultured butter
  • 1 charred pepper
  • Lime / salt
Preparation

Char the pepper. Cut corn from the cob. Cook hard and fast in butter until it catches at the edge. Fold in pepper, salt, and lime.
