Shop note / Why Rivet exists

We make the clothes we kept fixing.

Rivet started after years of patching blown knees, split pocket corners, and cuffs that quit before the person wearing them did. We cut every piece for reach, repetition, and repair—not a lifestyle photograph.

Designed in Ohio / Cut and sewn in small batches / Replacement hardware available / Repair guidance included with every garment
