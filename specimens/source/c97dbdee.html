The interior

Dot grid. Every page. All 192.

The dot grid gives structure without imposing it — guides for writing when you want them, invisible when you don’t. Charts, margins, sketches, and longhand all share the same page without negotiation.

Underneath, 100 gsm paper holds ink without ghosting through to the next page.

Interior specimen / actual page pattern one page, repeated 192 times

The page system

One pattern / three postures
  1. 01 Write

    Rows of dots keep lines of writing level without printing rules across the page.

  2. 02 Draw

    Dots behave like light construction lines for perspective, form, and spacing.

  3. 03 Connect

    Sketches and lists share one grid, so a page can hold a plan and its footnote together.
