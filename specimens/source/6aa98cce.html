03 — Origin information

Huila speaks through process.

Las Flores sits on the eastern slope of the Huila massif. Volcanic soil, shade-grown, hand-picked, fully washed and patio-dried.

Washed — clean, traced, cupped twice.

After picking, cherries are depulped, fermented, washed, and dried on patios. We cup the lot at origin with the producer, then again on arrival. Only lots that keep the apple-citrus clarity at our light profile become a release.

04 — Roasting philosophy
We roast light not as a style,
but as a record.
If the farm did the work,
the roast should not cover it.

Philosophy — Light as documentation

Roast to reveal, not to
impose. The lot decides
the profile.

We develop a target curve for each lot — charge, turn, first crack — then adjust for density and moisture. Light means we stop before origin character is muted. You taste Huila, not the roaster.
