Roasting
philosophy

A coffee with a known origin is a coffee with a fixed address. We buy from named farms at stated altitudes, roast to the process rather than a formula, and put every fact on the label where it belongs.

Las Flores is roasted light so the washed Caturra speaks first — orchard fruit up front, caramel underneath, citrus at the finish.

If we can’t name the farm, it doesn’t wear our name.
