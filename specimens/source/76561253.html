Interior — Dot grid at 5 mm

Light dots.
Strong page.

5 mm spacing is the system: rows for writing, columns for diagrams, points for alignment. Dots fade when you sketch, appear when you need a straight line.

Off-white 100 gsm keeps contrast soft on the eyes, firm under the pen. No feathering, no show-through that distracts on the reverse.
